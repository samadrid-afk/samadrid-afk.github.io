4: Rain World

Ok, you should really play this one, it makes me mad how people don't play it because they consider it too hard, but that's the point, Rain World is a game where you, a slugcat are dropped on a world you don't belong, the game since the first region shows you you aren't the protagonist, you are only trying to survive on a world as the same as the other enemies, The game tries to simulate a ecosystem where each enemy tries to survive even when you are not looking, going in groups or killing others for food, closely a alive world, i loved the ambient and all the moments i got inside the game because of this world.
