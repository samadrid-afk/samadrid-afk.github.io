3 Leches Cake

Tres Leches is one of the Best Desserts (on my opinion) Because of their taste having a texture similar to a sponge and having milk soaked on it, What they use to make the cake is a combination of 3 type of milks how the name says, evaporated milk, condensed milk, and whole milk. The cake is baked until light and fluffy, then poked with holes and drenched in the milk mixture before being chilled and topped with whipped cream. It is known for being incredibly moist and rich, with a light, airy texture that holds up well to the soaking liquid. 3 Leches is one of the most eaten desserts on all latin America and i eated a lot of them in celebrations with family.
