What is it?

Schizophrenia is a serious mental health disorder that affects how a person thinks, feels, and behaves. People with schizophrenia may experience symptoms such as hallucinations (seeing or hearing things that are not real), delusions (strong false beliefs), disorganized thinking or speech, and difficulty with motivation or emotions. These symptoms can make it hard to tell what is real

Fighting or managing Schizophrenia usually involves long-term treatment and support rather than a single cure. Doctors often prescribe antipsychotic medications to reduce symptoms like hallucinations and delusions, and therapy (such as cognitive behavioral therapy) can help people understand and cope with their thoughts. Support from family, counseling, and community programs can improve daily functioning, while healthy habits—like regular sleep, avoiding drugs or alcohol, managing stress, and staying socially connected—can also help keep symptoms more stable. Many people with schizophrenia live meaningful lives when they consistently follow treatment and have strong support
