A mental disorder is a health condition that affects a person's thinking, emotions, behavior, or mood in ways that cause significant distress or difficulty functioning in daily life. These conditions are studied in fields like Psychiatry and Clinical Psychology and can influence how someone relates to others, handles stress, or makes decisions. Mental disorders can be caused by a combination of biological factors (such as brain chemistry or genetics), psychological factors (like trauma or coping skills), and social or environmental influences. Examples include conditions such as Depression, Anxiety Disorders, and Schizophrenia.
